Shops and where they are

Standing in a shop, save the spot once — or paste where it is, for a shop you are not in. After that, opening the list there shows that shop's things. Your position is only compared on this phone; the shop spots are all rack keeps.

    Online shops and searches

    Mark a shop as online and its lines are ordered, then arrive, instead of being picked off a shelf — and it gets no spot to save or message on arrival. Give any shop its search address and every line for it gets a button that searches the shop for the part number, or the name. To find the address, search the shop's site for anything and copy what the browser shows, putting {q} where your words were — e.g. https://www.amazon.de/s?k={q}.

      Which shops sell which brand

      Which shops sell a brand: an item of that brand gets the shop filled in when you add it to the list, and when several shops sell it, each is a tap away. Add the brand again with another shop to list a second one. An item that names a store outright (e.g. "Farnell 876-7670") needs no rule.
