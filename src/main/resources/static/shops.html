Shops

Where each shop is, which ones you order from, and which shops sell which brand — what the shopping list uses to put each line under the right shops.

Where the shops are

Standing in a shop, save the spot once — from here, or with I'm at … — save this spot on the list — or paste where it is, for a shop you are not in. After that, opening the list there shows that shop's things. Your position is only compared on the phone; the shop spots are all rack keeps.

    Online shops and searches

    Mark a shop as online and its lines are ordered, then arrive, instead of being picked off a shelf — and it gets no spot to save or message on arrival. Give any shop its search address and every line for it gets a button that searches the shop for the name, or the part number. To find the address, search the shop's site for anything and copy what the browser shows, putting {q} where your words were — e.g. https://www.amazon.de/s?k={q}. Tap a shop to change it.

      Which shops sell which brand

      A line for something of that brand goes on the list for every shop that sells it — lines already on the list too. Add the brand again with another shop to list a second one. An item that names a shop outright (e.g. "Farnell 876-7670") needs no rule.
